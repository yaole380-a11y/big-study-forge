# Big Study Forge Demo Script

## Demo Goal

This demo shows how Big Study Forge helps learners process study materials using AI.

## Demo Scenario

A learner pastes a short piece of study material into Big Study Forge.

The system generates:

1. A short summary
2. Key concepts
3. Important points
4. Practice questions
5. Answer explanations
6. Review suggestions

## Example Input

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight to synthesize food from carbon dioxide and water. Photosynthesis generally involves the green pigment chlorophyll and generates oxygen as a byproduct.

## Expected AI Output

### Summary

Photosynthesis is the process where green plants use sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water to make food. Oxygen is produced as a byproduct.

### Key Concepts

- Photosynthesis
- Sunlight
- Carbon dioxide
- Water
- Chlorophyll
- Oxygen

### Practice Questions

1. What is photosynthesis?
2. What materials do plants need for photosynthesis?
3. What is produced as a byproduct?
4. Why is chlorophyll important?

### Review Suggestion

Review the relationship between sunlight, chlorophyll, carbon dioxide, water, glucose, and oxygen.
