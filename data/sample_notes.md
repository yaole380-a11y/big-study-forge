# Sample Learning Material

## Topic: Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight to make food from carbon dioxide and water.

This process usually takes place in the leaves of plants. Chlorophyll, the green pigment in leaves, helps plants absorb sunlight.

During photosynthesis, plants take in carbon dioxide from the air and water from the soil. With the help of sunlight, they produce glucose and oxygen.

The simplified formula for photosynthesis is:

Carbon dioxide + Water + Sunlight → Glucose + Oxygen

## Key Ideas

- Photosynthesis is important for plant growth.
- Chlorophyll helps plants absorb sunlight.
- Carbon dioxide and water are raw materials.
- Glucose is the food produced by plants.
- Oxygen is released as a byproduct.
- Photosynthesis supports life on Earth by producing oxygen.
